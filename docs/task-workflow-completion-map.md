future increments defined in the architecture. Their deferral does not justify

integration, finish scoped inputs and the addressed review loop, prove paired delivery
and process recovery, and run the final full Android gate. Keep later authorized
packages visible and assess them against the original specifications before declaring
the overall feature complete. PRs remain drafts while applicable gates are missing.
