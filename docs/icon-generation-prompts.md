# Icon generation provenance

Tool: built-in `image_gen`; no API-key CLI or model fallback. Reference:
`assets/icons/classic/icon.png` for the three new designs. The existing coral 3D
iOS icon from the parent commit was the cleanup reference for Mascot.

Shared generation constraints: one original Muqun mark, joined two-lobed m body,
one upward tuft on the upper left, rectangular eyes, triangular mouth, two short
feet; front-facing and readable at 48px; no lettering, franchise characters,
watermarks, extra accessories or rounded-square tile. Final transparent masters
were inspected before palette optimisation and platform export.

## Cyber — logo-brand

Create a square Muqun CYBER alternate icon aimed at cyberpunk/futuristic technology
aesthetics, evolved from Classic. Sleek dark midnight robotic enamel badge, sharp
clean cel-shaded planes, bold cyan rim accents, two magenta side panels, bright cyan
rectangular eyes and triangular mouth. Preserve Classic's recognisable shape.
Centered front-facing character, full tuft and feet visible, plain midnight navy
background, no scenery, particles, lettering or noisy texture. High contrast and
readable at 48px. One icon only.

Follow-up edit — background-extraction: remove the navy background to genuine
alpha. Preserve body surfaces, pose, cyan solid edge, facial geometry and magenta
panels. Remove exterior glow and cast shadow; no opaque matte, floor or fringe.
Keep the entire mascot inside transparent margins.

## Anime — logo-brand

Create one isolated transparent Muqun ANIME master for anime/chibi and kawaii
enthusiast personalisation. Retain the Classic silhouette and short feet. A joyful
chibi enamel-sticker illustration: strawberry pink body, lavender cel shadows,
dark rectangular eyes with a small square highlight, triangular smiling mouth,
restrained peach blush. Bold smooth plum outline, broad highlights, simple cel
shading. No human face, hair, clothing, accessories, flowers or separate decorative
shapes. No white sticker border, background, glow, cast shadow or text. Original
Muqun character, no imitation of an anime franchise.

## Arcade — logo-brand

Create one isolated transparent Muqun ARCADE master for retro-gaming and playful
Gen-Z digital culture tastes. Evolve the Classic identity into chunky pixel art
with a coarse 32–48-unit grid, stepped tuft and curves, dark purple outline,
saturated violet body, electric lime rectangular eyes and triangular mouth, and
two broad lavender highlights. An original collectible game sprite, not a known
game character. No dithering, screen, machine, surrounding symbols, shadow, glow,
text or tile. One complete front-facing mark.

## Mascot cleanup — background-extraction

Extract the existing coral 3D mascot to a clean transparent master. Preserve its
friendly two-lobed body, tuft, eyes, smile, cheeks, two feet, soft material and angle.
Remove the cream background and all floor/contact/cast shadow, including between
the feet. Keep the body opaque, with smooth antialiased edges and no pale matte,
dangling pixels or added outline. Full subject with transparent margins.

The graphite Focus and sage Bloom studies were superseded by the user's audience
clarification and are not project deliverables. Original generated renders remain
in the tool's local output cache; the optimised masters under `assets/icons/` are
the self-contained project inputs. The prompts above record the final production
briefs and extraction instructions; rerunning an image model is not byte-reproducible.
