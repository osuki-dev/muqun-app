# Muqun

Muqun connects your iPhone, iPad or Android device to the coding agents and
terminal sessions on your own computers. It works with Muqun Gateway, without
a Muqun account or hosted relay.

- Follow agent conversations and reply from your phone.
- Use terminals and browse files across your computers.
- Choose a home layout and theme that suit you.
- Dictate messages and preview audio in conversations.

Download the app from [muqun.dev](https://muqun.dev).
